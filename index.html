How statuses and fingerprint dates are interpreted

Complete, Completed, Approved, Cleared, Verified, Done, Yes and N/A are treated as satisfied. Need and empty cells are missing. Requested, Pending and Sent remain outstanding. Other values (including “x”) are flagged for review. An orientation date means scheduled, not completed.

Missing FP expiration includes blanks and values that are not valid dates, such as “Not in ch”. An expired date is shown separately. “FP Expiration”, “FP Expiration from CH” and “FP Expiration from ClearingHouse” map to the expiration field; the applicant detail shows the exact source column. A worksheet without that column is marked “No column”, not counted as a missing date.

Totals count worksheet records; the same applicant in two worksheets is retained twice with its source row. Updates belong in the original Excel file. Reopen it to refresh this view. Your workbook is held in memory and cleared when this page is closed or reloaded.
